NetSurf for TI-Nspire

Getting online

Plug the calculator into a computer with the USB cable. It shows up there as a USB network adapter (Linux, Windows 10/11). Share the computer's Internet connection with it, and the status bar shows the calculator's address. Without sharing, sites on the computer itself are at http://pc/.
